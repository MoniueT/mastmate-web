The star feature

The 48-hour trigger correlator

Tag foods and everyday triggers, heat, stress, fragrance, poor sleep, in a 60-second daily check-in. MastMate compares each one's 48-hour reaction rate against your calm-day baseline and tells you what's worth a closer look and what's looking friendly, with honest confidence labels: strong signal, building, or early. A pattern analyzer that ignores the delay blames the wrong food. This one doesn't.

Live, not retrospective

Your histamine bucket, live

The community's favourite way to understand MCAS, finally on a gauge. A live estimate of your current load from the last 48 hours of foods and triggers, four zones from plenty of room to near the brim, showing exactly what's in the bucket right now, not just what happened last week.

Guided, not guesswork

3-day reintroduction wizard

A small portion, then normal, then observe, structured over three days so a reintroduction actually tells you something instead of muddying the water. Every result feeds your personal food map, so the freshness-aware food list adapts as you mark things safe or trigger for you.

When it matters most

A reaction plan you can actually follow

Calm, unambiguous steps with emergency signs, adrenaline-pen aware, plus a rescue playbook ranked from what actually helped you on past reaction days. Printable wallet cards cover a medical emergency card and your own safe-foods card, so the plan is there even when your phone isn't.

Built from your own check-ins, the summary lays out the multi-system evidence pattern clinicians actually look for. It's also where the tryptase truth insight lives: normal tryptase but still reacting is common in MCAS, and your log is what makes that case credible instead of anecdotal.
